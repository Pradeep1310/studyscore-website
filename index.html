Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About StudyScore

What is StudyScore?

StudyScore is India's first independent, student-powered rating system for study abroad agents and overseas education consultants. Think of it as CIBIL for education agents — a transparent, data-driven quality score based on real student experiences. We are not affiliated with any education agency.

Is my survey response anonymous?

Yes. The survey does not ask for your name or email address and does not require a login. If you choose to attach an optional verification document, it is stored privately, used only to verify that the review is genuine, and never published or shared with any agency.

How is StudyScore different from Google Reviews?

Google Reviews can be manipulated — agents often ask staff and friends to post fake positive reviews. StudyScore collects structured feedback on specific parameters, counts only verified reviews, weights recent experiences more heavily, and applies a Bayesian stabilisation formula that resists review-bombing and artificial rank manipulation.

Is StudyScore affiliated with any education agency?

No. StudyScore is completely independent. We are not affiliated with, sponsored by, or funded by any education agent, university, or recruitment company. Our only stakeholder is the student and their family.

How will study abroad agents be rated?

Each education agent receives a StudyScore from 0 to 100 based on verified student feedback across five parameters: fee transparency (20%), communication quality (15%), visa support quality (25%), post-visa support (10%), and overall student satisfaction (30%). Recent reviews carry more weight than old ones, and a minimum number of verified reviews is required before any score is displayed.

Why do some agent profiles show no metrics yet?

Provisional profiles are compiled from publicly available information and remain unclaimed and unverified. StudyScore never publishes estimated or invented figures. Scores and metrics appear only after enough verified, anonymous student reviews have been collected for that agency.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

What we collect

The StudyScore survey is anonymous by design. It does not ask for your name or email address and does not require a login. Responses are stored securely in our own database. Individual survey responses are never published and are never shared with any agency in identifiable form. Only aggregated, anonymised findings and scores appear publicly.

If you choose to attach an optional verification document (such as an offer letter or fee receipt), it is stored privately, used only to verify that the review is genuine, and never published or shared with any agency. We retain a cryptographic fingerprint (SHA-256 hash) of the file to block duplicate submissions. Images may be compressed on your device before upload.

If you submit an inquiry form on an agent profile page, you choose to share the details you enter (such as your name, contact information, and message). We use that information only to route and respond to your inquiry through our lead management system. We do not sell personal data.

Cookies and third parties

This site does not set its own tracking cookies and does not run third-party advertising trackers. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, loads fonts from Google Fonts, and stores survey data with our database provider (Supabase); those providers may receive standard technical request data (such as IP address and browser type) under their own privacy policies.

Corrections and removal

To request correction or deletion of information you have submitted, including any uploaded document, email pradeep@studyscore.in. We respond to all genuine requests.
