Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About StudyScore

What is StudyScore?

StudyScore is India's first independent, student-powered rating system for study abroad agents and overseas education consultants. Think of it as CIBIL for education agents — a transparent, data-driven quality score based on real student experiences. We are not affiliated with any education agency.

Is my survey response anonymous?

Yes, 100% anonymous. We do not collect your name, email, or any identifying information. No study abroad agent will ever know who submitted a specific response. Your privacy is absolute.

How is StudyScore different from Google Reviews?

Google Reviews can be manipulated — agents often ask staff and friends to post fake positive reviews. StudyScore collects structured, anonymous feedback on specific parameters like fee transparency, communication quality, and post-visa support. The data is aggregated across many responses to create a quality score that cannot be gamed by agents.

Is StudyScore affiliated with any education agency?

No. StudyScore is completely independent. We are not affiliated with, sponsored by, or funded by any education agent, university, or recruitment company. Our only stakeholder is the student and their family.

How will study abroad agents be rated?

Each education agent receives a StudyScore from 0 to 100 based on verified student feedback across five parameters: fee transparency (20%), communication quality (15%), visa support quality (25%), post-visa support (10%), and overall student satisfaction (30%). Higher scores indicate better student experiences.

Why do some agent profiles show no metrics yet?

Provisional profiles are compiled from publicly available information and remain unclaimed and unverified. StudyScore never publishes estimated or invented figures. Metrics such as visa success rates appear only after enough verified, anonymous student reviews have been collected for that agency.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

What we collect

The StudyScore survey is anonymous by design. It does not ask for your name or email address, does not require a login, and responses are stored securely with our form provider (Google Forms). Individual survey responses are never published and are never shared with any agency in identifiable form. Only aggregated, anonymised findings may appear in public reports.

If you submit an inquiry form on an agent profile page, you choose to share the details you enter (such as your name, contact information, and message). We use that information only to route and respond to your inquiry through our lead management system. We do not sell personal data.

Cookies and third parties

This site does not set its own tracking cookies and does not run third-party advertising trackers.
